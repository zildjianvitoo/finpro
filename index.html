Getting started

  1. Import a song from your Files. Choose the section you want to cover.
  2. Put on headphones. Recording needs them so the song does not leak into your vocal.
  3. Sing along with the pitch guide and lyrics. The song keeps playing from one part to the next.
  4. Review your clips. Retake any clip you are not happy with. Verca combines the clips into one cover video you can save to Photos.

Frequently asked questions

Why are headphones required?
The song plays while you sing. Without headphones the microphone would also record the song.

Why can't I see lyrics for my song?
Lyrics are looked up by song title, artist and length. If no synchronised version matches your file, the app records without lyrics.

Does Retake include songs?
No. You import songs from your own files. You are responsible for having the right to use them.

Camera, microphone or Photos access was denied.
Open Settings > Retake and turn the permission on. Verca uses the camera and microphone to record, and Photos access only to save your finished video.
