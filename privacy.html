Privacy Policy

Retake: Cover Studio · Last updated 16 November 2026

Retake is an iPhone app for recording song covers. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your recordings stay on your device. This page explains the few cases where information leaves it.

What the app uses and why

  • Camera and microphone – to record your cover video and to check your pitch while you sing. Processing happens on your device.
  • Songs you import – audio files you choose from Files. They are copied into the app's private storage. Vocal separation and pitch analysis run on your device.
  • Photos (add only) – to save your finished cover video when you ask. Retake cannot read your photo library.

Information sent off your device

  • Song recognition (Apple ShazamKit). To find the title and artist of an imported song, Retake sends Apple an audio fingerprint (a signature) of short excerpts, not the audio itself. Apple's handling is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Lyrics (LRCLIB). To show synchronised lyrics, Retake sends the song's title, artist and duration to lrclib.net. No audio, account or device identifier is sent.

If you are offline, both lookups are skipped and the app still works without them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.
  • We do not track you, show ads, or sell or share data for advertising.
  • We do not upload your recordings or videos. They are shared only if you share them yourself.

Storage and deletion

Everything the app creates (imported songs, takes, drafts, finished videos, settings) is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Videos you saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there.

Songs and copyright

Retake does not provide songs. You are responsible for having the right to use the songs you import and to publish covers you make.

Children

Retake does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
